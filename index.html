Pressings

styles, drawn in light, mixed live

Two families, eleven styles, played on real recorded instruments. Pick a style and a DJ mixes it in on the next bar, the way it's done in a booth: beat-matched, bass swapped on the downbeat, filtered, pitched, or cut on the one.

One ring per role. Each bar's hits join into the shape of its rhythm; while two records play, you see both.
